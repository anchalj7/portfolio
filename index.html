Full-Stack Developer

Anchal Jaiswal is a passionate and dedicated B.Tech student in Computer Science and Engineering at Lovely Professional University, with a strong interest in full-stack web development, artificial intelligence, and building technology-driven solutions for real-world challenges.

She possesses a solid foundation in programming languages including Java, JavaScript, C, and C++, along with expertise in modern web technologies such as React, Next.js, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, Tailwind CSS, HTML, CSS, and MySQL.

Currently, Anchal is pursuing a MERN Stack Development with AI Internship, where she is enhancing her skills in full-stack application development by integrating AI-powered features into modern web applications.

During this internship, she developed a Disaster Relief Coordination Platform, a comprehensive web application designed to streamline emergency response by enabling incident reporting, volunteer coordination, resource management, and real-time communication.

Beyond academics, Anchal actively participates in hackathons and technical competitions. She participated in FullStack Intelligence 1.0, organized by LPU in collaboration with Unstop, where her team secured the 2nd Runner-Up position among numerous talented participants.

She has also developed impactful projects such as Lexora — a legal consultation platform, and CharityLoom — a donation platform connecting donors with NGOs for essential resources.

Committed to continuous learning and professional growth, Anchal enjoys exploring emerging technologies, participating in coding competitions, and collaborating on innovative projects.
