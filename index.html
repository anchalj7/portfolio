About Me

Full Stack Developer

Anchal Jaiswal is a passionate and dedicated B.Tech student in Computer Science and Engineering at Lovely Professional University, with a strong interest in full-stack web development, artificial intelligence, and building technology-driven solutions for real-world challenges.

She possesses a solid foundation in programming languages including Java, JavaScript, and C, along with expertise in modern web technologies such as React, Next.js, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, Tailwind CSS, HTML, CSS, and MySQL. Currently, Anchal is pursuing a MERN Stack Development with AI Internship, where she is enhancing her skills in full-stack application development by integrating AI-powered features into modern web applications.

During this internship, she developed a Disaster Relief Coordination Platform, a comprehensive web application designed to streamline emergency response by enabling incident reporting, volunteer coordination, resource management, and real-time communication.

Beyond academics, Anchal actively participates in hackathons and technical competitions. She participated in FullStack Intelligence 1.0, organized by Lovely Professional University (LPU) in collaboration with Unstop, where her team secured the 2nd Runner-Up position among numerous talented participants.

She has also developed impactful projects such as CharityLoom, a donation platform that connects donors with NGOs for food, clothing, wheelchairs, and other essential resources.

Committed to continuous learning and professional growth, Anchal enjoys exploring emerging technologies, participating in coding competitions, and collaborating on innovative projects. With a combination of technical expertise, creativity, and a strong desire to solve real-world problems, she aspires to contribute to impactful software solutions while continuously expanding her knowledge in full-stack development and AI.
