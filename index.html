Full-Stack Developer

Hi, I’m Anchal Jaiswal, a Computer Science student at Lovely Professional University and a developer who enjoys turning ideas into real, usable experiences. My journey started with a simple curiosity — how does an idea on paper become something people can actually interact with? That curiosity gradually led me into web development, AI, and building technology-driven solutions.

I started my programming journey with C, C++, and Java, and later explored modern web development with React, Next.js, Node.js, databases, and AI. With every project, I have learned something new about both technology and problem-solving.

I have worked on projects like CharityLoom, a platform focused on connecting donors with NGOs, and a Disaster Relief Coordination Platform designed to make emergency response more organized.

Beyond development, I enjoy hackathons, exploring new technologies, and working on ideas that challenge me to think differently. My team also secured the 2nd Runner-Up position in FullStack Intelligence 1.0 by LPU × Unstop.

I’m still exploring, experimenting, and improving with every project. For me, development is not just about writing code — it’s about learning, creating, and turning ideas into something meaningful. 🚀
